Your score

How to train, and how this keeps score honestly

  1. Start coarse. Guess the element of the rising sign before the exact sign. Pure guessing gets about 1 in 4, so real skill would show up sooner.
  2. Chance is worked out for you. At European latitudes some signs rise much more often than others (Virgo and Libra rise slowly, so they're common). Each guess counts as lucky in proportion to how common that answer is in the deck, so always picking the most common sign doesn't count as skill.
  3. The birth day is hidden. The date would give away the Sun sign and narrow down the Moon. You see the birth year, which only fixes the slow planets.
  4. Give it 100 guesses. Short runs swing wildly. If you do beat chance, start over and see if a fresh 100 does it again. One good run in twenty happens by luck.
  5. With people you know, you already know their birthday, so the Sun sign is left out of their charts. The rising sign's luck comes from how often each sign rises at their birthplace's latitude.
  6. "Pick the real chart" is the format of the Carlson (1985) and Vernon Clark studies: one real chart and two decoys (from other people in the deck, or for your own people, the sky over their birthplace at random other times). Luck gets exactly 1 in 3.

Your guesses stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
